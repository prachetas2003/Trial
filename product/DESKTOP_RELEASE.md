Buildlings 0.3.0 introduces a real 3D desktop companion: original sculpted
geometry, skinned flexible ears, expressive eyes, and articulated paws.

Sprout wanders along curved paths, occasionally chases your cursor, crouches
before jumping, compresses on landing, and relaxes into cursor strokes with
happy eyes and 3D sparkles. Use **Say hello** in the notification-area menu
to make it reach a paw toward you. Idle breathing, blinks, and ear follow-through
continue between activities. Ember and Orbit share the rig with colour and
ear variations.

The transparent native window stays stationary over its display. A small
320 × 370 WebGL canvas draws the pet; display-synchronized frames drive both
its travel and character animation, with interpolated 120 Hz motion physics.
Clicks pass through, and the companion does not take keyboard focus.

Download **Buildlings-Windows-x64.zip**, extract the entire archive, and open
**Buildlings.exe**. Quit the previous version from its tray menu before
opening this update. No Node.js, AI API key, or paid asset service is required.

This is an unsigned Windows 10/11 x64 preview and requires WebGL 2 graphics
support. SHA256SUMS.txt contains the archive checksum. Windows native checks
must pass before this workflow publishes a release. Actual frame rates depend
on your display, graphics hardware, and other running applications.
