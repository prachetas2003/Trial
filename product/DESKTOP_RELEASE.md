Buildlings 0.9.0 synchronizes focus controls and adds quieter appearance polish and brief celebrations.

- Toolbar and Focus panel receive one versioned snapshot. Stale responses cannot
  roll back a newer pause/resume. The panel remains updated in the background.
- Resume restores automatic edge tucking. Hovering or petting cannot hold the
  return movement in place. Explicit hiding and continuous chase remain choices.
- Speech bubbles scale with monkey size, with readable minimum text, and use the
  screen's selected Light/Dark style. Auto recognises system theme or white ink.
- Appearance has Focus edge coverage (64–280 px), independent of strength. Narrow
  gradient strips still leave the centre clear and avoid a full-screen shadow layer.
- Browser setup starts at chrome://extensions / edge://extensions, Developer mode,
  Load unpacked, then the app's FocusShield subfolder. Folder opening is optional.
  Clear section labels explain Focus, Blocking, Appearance and Notes; presets can
  be customized with individual switches and settings.
- Small confetti bursts beside the monkey mark 25%, 50% and 75% of active focus
  time. Completed focus brings the monkey back and plays a brief screen burst.
  Milestones do not repeat after pause/recovery. Early Finish and breaks do not
  trigger screen celebration. The independent switch is off in Blocker Only.
- Confetti is click-through, capped at 48 small pieces, and removed within 2.4
  seconds. Reduced motion uses a small acknowledgement instead of moving pieces.
- Safe Mode, asynchronous atomic saves, visible failures, native timer hiding and
  renderer recovery from 0.8.2 remain included.

Quit the old app, extract the entire ZIP into a fresh folder and run Buildlings.exe,
or Start-Safe-Mode.cmd if that is the working startup path on your machine.
Saved notes, history and pairing stay shared. FocusShield remains 0.8.0; an
already updated extension needs no reinstall for these changes.
Windows 10/11 x64 preview. SHA256SUMS.txt accompanies the archive.
