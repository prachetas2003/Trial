Buildlings 0.5.0 turns **Mischief** into a little focus companion.

- **Quotes:** 90 original warm lines and 35 sourced quotations from investors,
  founders, inventors, and scientists. Speech bubbles fill gently; attributed
  quotes show only the quote and name. Decks avoid repeats until exhausted.
- **Pomodoro:** choose a work intention, focus length, and break length. Pause,
  resume, or finish early. Every fourth completed session offers a longer break.
  The next session starts when you choose it.
- **Hide and peek:** tuck the monkey to the screen edge, let it wander, or hide
  it completely. Focus sessions tuck it away automatically by default.
- **Keep my thoughts:** save a thought without following the distraction.
  Return to it after the session, mark it reviewed, or remove it.
- **I’m staying:** record a deliberate choice with a button or Ctrl+Alt+S.
  Mischief responds with a little sparkle and a sourced quote. The session
  summary shows timer time, your intention, and the choices you recorded.

Double-click the notification-area icon or press **Ctrl+Alt+F** to open your
focus corner. **Ctrl+Alt+T** opens thought capture. **Ctrl+Alt+H** switches
between wandering and peeking. The focus corner is an ordinary closable
window; the monkey and speech remain on a transparent, click-through surface.

Ordinary quotes stay quiet during focus by default. Change this in the focus
corner. No browser blocking, screen reading, AI API, account, or subscription
is required. Thoughts, the last 50 sessions, marker preferences, and a paused
timer checkpoint stay in local app-data. Timer time is not a measurement of
attention, and the choice count records your presses. Closing the app, sleep,
and screen lock pause the timer; restarting restores a paused checkpoint.

Download **Buildlings-Windows-x64.zip**, extract the entire archive, and open
**Buildlings.exe**. Quit the previous Buildlings version from its tray menu
before opening this update. `QUOTE_SOURCES.md` contains the quote references.

This is an unsigned Windows 10/11 x64 preview. SHA256SUMS.txt contains the
archive checksum. Native Windows checks must pass before publishing.
Actual frame rates and battery use depend on your hardware and other apps.
