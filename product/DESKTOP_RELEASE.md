Buildlings 0.4.0 introduces **Mischief**, an original marker-doodle monkey.

Thick variable-width strokes, fixed ink texture, small glossy highlights,
round ears, a scribbled orange shirt, and a curled tail make it feel drawn
on your screen. The face and the space between strokes remain transparent.
Choose **White marker — for dark screens** from the notification-area menu
when using a dark desktop or editor. This preview contains one doodle character.

Mischief wanders, chases your cursor, crouches before jumping, settles on
landing, and leans into cursor strokes with happy eyes and drawn sparkles.
**Say hello** makes its hand come forward and grow through foreshortening.
Head turns and tail motion follow smoothly, including interrupted gestures.

A transparent native window stays stationary over its display. A small
320 × 370 Canvas 2D surface draws the monkey at up to 2× pixel resolution.
Display-synchronized frames drive travel and pose together, with interpolated
120 Hz motion physics. Clicks pass through without taking keyboard focus.
The monkey does not require WebGL, paid assets, accounts, or AI API keys.

Download **Buildlings-Windows-x64.zip**, extract the entire archive, and open
**Buildlings.exe**. Quit the previous Buildlings version from its tray menu
before opening this update.

This is an unsigned Windows 10/11 x64 preview. SHA256SUMS.txt contains the
archive checksum. Native Windows checks must pass before a release is
published. Actual frame rates depend on the display, hardware, and other apps.

Browser preview: https://prachetas2003.github.io/Trial/roam/?v=0.4.0
The browser monkey stays inside the page; the Windows app roams over other apps.
