## H036 — James Clear

Source: https://jamesclear.com/quotes/you-do-not-rise-to-the-level-of-your-goals-you-fall-to-the-level-of-your-systems

The line is published verbatim in Clear’s own quote archive.

## H037–H038 — Sam Altman

Source: https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful

Both lines appear in the section titled “Focus.” H037 is the section’s opening
sentence. H038 is the following paragraph’s statement contrasting the right work
with simply working more hours.

## H039 — Richard Feynman

Source: https://calteches.library.caltech.edu/684/1/ES65.2.2002.pdf

Caltech’s *Engineering & Science* reproduces the phrase “What I cannot create,
I do not understand” from the blackboard left in Feynman’s office.

## H040–H041 — Paul Graham

Source: https://www.paulgraham.com/greatwork.html

H040 is the essay’s closing challenge. H041 appears in Graham’s discussion of
starting small and iterating toward great work.

## H042 — Richard Hamming

Source: https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~robins/YouAndYourResearch.html

The sentence appears in Hamming’s discussion of deliberately working on
important problems. “Important” in that passage means a problem with a
reasonable line of attack, not merely a prestigious topic.

## H043 — James Dyson

Source: https://media.dyson.com/images_resize_sites/inside_dyson/assets/uk/downloads/rolls_royce_speech.pdf

Dyson describes the development of his first vacuum and says he made 5,127
“mistakes,” or “prototypes.” The bank preserves the source’s hyphenated phrasing
and quoted word “prototypes.”
