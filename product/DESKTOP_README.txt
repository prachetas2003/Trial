BUILDLINGS — MISCHIEF MARKER MONKEY PREVIEW 0.4.0

1. Quit any older Buildlings instance from its notification-area menu.
2. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. Keep all its files together.
3. Open Buildlings.exe. No Node.js, account, or API key is needed.
4. Move your cursor gently across the monkey to pet it.
5. Right-click the Buildlings icon in the Windows notification area
   (it may be under the hidden-icons arrow) for controls.
   Choose "White marker — for dark screens" if your desktop is dark.
   Choose "Say hello" to see its hand reach toward you.
   You can pause, turn chasing/jumps off, bring it to this screen, hide, or quit.

Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+Alt+P pause; Ctrl+Alt+H hide; Ctrl+Alt+Q quit.
If a shortcut is used by another app, use the notification-area menu.

The monkey lets mouse clicks pass through and does not take keyboard focus.
It does not read your editor, record keystrokes, use AI credits, send cursor
positions, sample your screen, or run on startup. Cursor position is used
locally for animation. Marker and motion preferences are stored in app-data.

The original marker artwork is drawn on a transparent Canvas 2D surface.
It has variable-width strokes, stable ink grain, small highlights, a scribbled
orange shirt, and movable arms, legs, head, and tail. Empty areas show your
screen through the drawing. Walking, chasing, jumping, petting, and reaching
blend smoothly. WebGL is not required by the monkey renderer.

This is an unsigned preview for 64-bit Windows 10/11. Windows may show an
unrecognized-app warning. Only download from the project's official GitHub
release, check its SHA256SUMS.txt, and follow your Windows policy.

The browser preview stays within the page; the Windows application is
the version that moves over other applications.
