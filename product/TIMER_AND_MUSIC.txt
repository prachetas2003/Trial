MISCHIEF 0.11.1 — TIMER & BACKGROUND MUSIC

Close the old app using its tray menu > Quit Buildlings. Extract this whole ZIP
into a new folder and launch Buildlings.exe. Your saved sessions and settings
remain in the same app-data location.

MOVE THE TIMER
Drag the dotted grip at the left end of the floating timer. Release to save its
position. The padlock locks or unlocks moving; it never locks the other buttons.
Controls > Appearance > Reset timer position brings the bar back to the top.
The tray menu also offers Show timer bar and Reset timer position.

BACKGROUND MUSIC
Click the musical-note button on the floating timer, or switch Music on in the
controls panel. Set the volume under Focus timer > Background music.
The tray menu also has a Background music switch.
Music starts off by default, loops offline, and keeps playing when the panel or
monkey is hidden. Pause/resume of the focus timer does not interrupt your music.
Turning Music off pauses the track; turning it back on continues playback.
Computer sleep pauses playback; it resumes on wake if Music is still enabled.

BASE VERSION
This update uses the modified portable release supplied by the owner, asset
Buildlings-Windows-x64.current.1.zip from release 334455667788. All original
runtime files, FocusShield files, and native-pet helpers are preserved byte for
byte; only resources/app.asar is replaced. Backup ASAR files remain untouched.
